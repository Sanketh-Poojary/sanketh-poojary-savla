Next-Generation Sample Quality Control

Precision microfluidics for the automated analysis of DNA, RNA, proteins, and cells. Accelerate your genomic and proteomic workflows with highly reliable sizing and quantification.

Analytical Applications

DNA Analysis

High-resolution sizing and quantification of DNA fragments. Perfect for Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) library prep, PCR fragment analysis, and restriction digests.

RNA Analysis

Objective evaluation of RNA quality using the industry-standard RNA Integrity Number (RIN). Ensure the highest quality data for microarrays and RT-qPCR.

Protein Analysis

Rapid and sensitive sizing and quantification of proteins. An automated alternative to traditional SDS-PAGE gels with digital, reproducible results.

Powered by Microfluidics

Our bioanalyzer utilizes lab-on-a-chip technology, reducing sample handling and minimizing the risk of contamination. By integrating separation, staining, and detection into a single automated step, we deliver reproducible data in a fraction of the time.

  • Minimal Sample Volume: Requires only 1 µL of sample.
  • Fast Turnaround: Analysis complete in 30-40 minutes.
  • Digital Data: Easily export electropherograms and gel-like images.
  • High Sensitivity: Detect picogram concentrations of nucleic acids.
